Automated duty responds to operational events and performs periodic patrols. It uses a configured model and can execute tools within its assigned role.

## Configure

1. Prepare a working [model](/docs/guides/automation/models/).
2. Choose a duty role, policy, patrol interval, and notification language.
3. Enable duty and configure recipient channels.
4. Inspect subsequent events, analysis, and notifications.
5. Verify any resulting operation against actual platform state.

Defaults are Reader, NearCritical, a 30-minute patrol interval, and English notifications. The interval accepts 15 minutes to 24 hours. Event-driven analysis does not wait for the next patrol.

Policies include EarlyRisk, NearCritical, and LimitOrFailure. They describe when the agent should act on risk; they are not one universal fixed numeric threshold for every resource.

## Permissions and recipients

Reader provides observation. Operator and Admin allow unattended writes within their platform permissions. Duty has no personal account scope and does not act as a selected human user.

Recipients must have enabled the relevant notification channel and have a role at least as high as the duty role. For example, Operator duty notifications go to eligible Operators and Admins, not Readers.

## Verify and troubleshoot

Check duty configuration, model errors, recent events, channel delivery, subscriptions, and role filtering. An absent notification does not prove no problem exists.

Disabling duty does not disable ordinary operational alerts. User conversations follow the user's language; duty notifications use the configured notification language. See [notification troubleshooting](/docs/guides/automation/im/notifications/).
