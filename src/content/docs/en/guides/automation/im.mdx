Connect messaging channels to CDelivery for conversations and operational notifications.

1. As an administrator, open **Settings → Channels** and choose Slack, Telegram, Discord, Lark, or Feishu.
2. Enter the bot or application credentials requested on the page and save.
3. Open the same channel under **Account** and bind your own immutable user ID as instructed.
4. After configuring the [AI assistant](/docs/guides/automation/), send the bot a private message to start a conversation.
5. Enable notifications on your account's channel page as needed; set the notification language under **Settings → Agent → Duty**.

Account binding and notifications are configured separately.
